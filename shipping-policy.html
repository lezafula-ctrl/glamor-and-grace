Shipping & Delivery Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

We deliver orders within South Africa using the delivery option selected at checkout. Please check that your contact details and delivery or collection information are correct before paying.

1. Delivery options and fees

Delivery optionFee
PAXI — standardR150
PAXI — large / heavyR200
Courier Guy — standardR200
Courier Guy — large / heavyR250

These are the currently listed fees. The final delivery fee shown at checkout applies to your order. If a delivery option is unavailable for an item or destination, we will contact you before proceeding.

2. Processing and estimated delivery

Orders are processed after successful payment is confirmed. Many products are sourced from suppliers after a customer places an order, so we need time to order and receive the item before dispatch or handover to the delivery provider.

Our current estimated delivery timeframe is approximately 14–21 business days. This is an estimate, not a guaranteed arrival date. Supplier availability, public holidays, courier or collection-point operations, weather and other events outside our reasonable control can cause delays. We will communicate material updates when we receive them.

3. Delivery details

For PAXI, provide your preferred PAXI collection point and collect your parcel according to the collection provider's notification and requirements. For Courier Guy, provide a complete and accurate delivery address and any useful access instructions. Please monitor messages from the delivery provider and collect parcels promptly.

4. Tracking and delivery issues

Where tracking information is available, we will share it or provide an order update. If a parcel appears delayed, lost, damaged in transit or delivered to the wrong place, contact us at glamourandgrace07@gmail.com with your order reference. We will investigate with the relevant delivery provider.

5. Incorrect addresses and missed collection

Customers are responsible for checking that the delivery or collection details supplied at checkout are accurate. Incorrect details or failure to collect a parcel may cause delays or additional charges from the delivery provider. We will discuss any available options and any additional charges before arranging a new delivery where possible.

6. Contact

For delivery questions, email glamourandgrace07@gmail.com and include your order reference.

Important: This policy does not exclude rights or remedies available to customers under applicable South African law.

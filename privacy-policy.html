Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

Glamor & Grace respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you browse our website, place an order or contact us. We aim to handle personal information in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) and other applicable law.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect information you provide such as your name, email address, telephone number, delivery address or selected collection point, order details, product options and messages you send us. We may also receive payment confirmation or a transaction reference from a payment provider. We do not need your card PIN or online banking password.

2. How we use information

  • To receive, process and manage orders.
  • To confirm payment and arrange supplier fulfilment, delivery or collection.
  • To answer questions, send order updates and handle complaints or returns.
  • To maintain business records, prevent fraud and comply with legal obligations.
  • To maintain and improve website operation and security.

3. Suppliers and service providers

Because some products are sourced from suppliers after an order is placed, we may need to share the minimum necessary order or delivery information with a supplier or delivery provider to fulfil that order. Payment processing may be handled by a third-party payment provider such as PayFast. These providers handle information under their own terms and privacy practices.

4. Storage and security

We take reasonable steps to protect information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access. Order and website data may be stored using third-party services that support our website. No online system can be guaranteed completely secure, so please do not send passwords, card PINs or unnecessary sensitive information to us.

5. Retention

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed for the purposes described in this policy, including order fulfilment, customer support, accounting, dispute resolution and legal requirements. Retention periods may differ depending on the type of record and applicable law.

6. Your choices and rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access to personal information we hold about you, ask for correction of inaccurate information, raise an objection where legally available, or ask a question about how we use your information. We may need to verify your identity before responding and may retain some records where the law requires it.

7. Cookies and website services

Our website and its hosting or technical services may use essential browser storage or similar technologies to support features such as the shopping cart, sign-in and website operation. Third-party services may process technical information according to their own policies.

8. Children

Our website is intended for general shoppers and is not designed to knowingly collect children's personal information without appropriate authority or consent where required by law.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website or practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email glamorandgrace@gmail.com.

Note: This policy should be reviewed if the business's data practices or contact details change. It is a practical summary, not legal advice.

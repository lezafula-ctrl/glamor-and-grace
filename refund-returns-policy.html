Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

We want you to receive the item you ordered. If your order arrives damaged, faulty or incorrect, contact us so we can review the issue and help resolve it in line with applicable South African consumer-protection law.

1. Check your order

Please check your parcel and items as soon as reasonably possible after delivery or collection. If there is a problem, contact us at glamourandgrace07@gmail.com. Include your order reference, a description of the problem and clear photographs where helpful. Please keep the item and packaging until we advise you on the next steps.

2. Damaged, faulty or incorrect items

We will review the details and explain the next steps. Depending on the circumstances and your legal rights, a resolution may include repair, replacement, correction of the order or a refund. We may ask for reasonable information to assess the claim. Your rights under the Consumer Protection Act and other applicable laws are not reduced by this policy.

3. Change-of-mind requests

Contact us before returning an item if you have changed your mind. A change-of-mind return is not automatically guaranteed for every product or situation. Any applicable statutory cancellation or cooling-off rights will be respected, including where specific rules apply to online or direct-marketing transactions. We will explain any lawful conditions that apply to your request.

4. Items sourced after purchase

Some products are ordered from our suppliers after payment is received. This does not remove your legal rights. If a supplier cannot fulfil a paid order, we will contact you and discuss an appropriate solution, including a refund where applicable.

5. Refund processing

Where a refund is due, we will confirm the agreed resolution and process it using an appropriate payment method. The time it takes for the funds to reflect may depend on the payment provider or bank. We will not ask for your banking password, card PIN or one-time password to process a refund.

6. Return shipping

We will explain how an item should be returned, if a return is required, after reviewing the issue. Responsibility for reasonable return costs will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the circumstances of the case. Please do not send items to a supplier or address without instructions from us.

7. Contact us

Email glamourandgrace07@gmail.com with the subject line “Return or Refund Request” and include your order reference.

Please note: This policy does not remove or limit consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under South African law.
